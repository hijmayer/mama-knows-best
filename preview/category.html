Baby & Toddler Sleep

Sleep was the thing that nearly broke me as a new mom, and the thing every parent I know asks about first. The truth is that baby sleep isn't one problem, it's a moving target: what works at three weeks is useless at four months, and just when you've got a rhythm, a regression arrives to humble you.

These guides walk through each stage in order, newborn patterns (spoiler: chaos is normal), safe sleep setup per AAP guidelines, sleep training when you're ready (and only if you want to), and the toddler bedtime negotiations that come after.
