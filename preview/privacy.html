Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Mama Knows Best respects your privacy. This policy explains what information this site collects and how it is used.

Information you choose to share

If you send a message through the contact form, we receive your name, email address and message so we can reply. Contact form messages are delivered by FormSubmit, a form-handling service. If you sign up for the newsletter, your email address is stored with our email provider and used only to send you the newsletter. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email.

Information collected automatically

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical information such as your IP address when you visit, for security and reliability. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address in order to deliver them. We do not run advertising, and we do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Cookies

Mama Knows Best does not set cookies of its own. Sites you visit from our links, such as retailers, may use their own cookies under their own policies.

Affiliate links

Some product links may be affiliate links. If you click one and make a purchase, the retailer may use cookies to credit the referral. We never receive your personal or payment information from those purchases.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site grows, for example if we add privacy-friendly analytics or begin offering our own products. The date above shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Reach out through the contact form.
