Baby Gear & Safety

The best baby products, safety guides, and everything you need to keep your baby safe and comfortable.

The baby industry wants you to believe you need everything. You don't. After registering for far too much and watching half of it collect dust, I've learned the difference between gear that earns its space and gear that just looked good in the registry checklist.

These guides focus on the decisions that actually matter, the car seat (the one purchase where safety standards from NHTSA should drive your choice), the stroller you'll push for years, and baby-proofing once your little one is on the move. Borrow before you buy, skip the gimmicks, and spend where it counts.
