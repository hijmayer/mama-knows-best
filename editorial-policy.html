Editorial Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Our Mission

Mama Knows Best exists to give new and expecting parents warm, practical, judgment-free guidance for the everyday challenges of raising little ones — grounded in reputable sources and real lived experience.

How Our Content Is Created

Articles on this site are written and edited by Jordan, the founder of Mama Knows Best, drawing on personal parenting experience and research into guidance published by trusted medical organizations. Writing and editing tools, including AI-assisted drafting tools, may be used in the preparation of articles; every article is personally reviewed, edited, and fact-checked against reputable sources before publication.

Our Sources

Wherever our articles touch on health or safety, we align our guidance with — and link directly to — authoritative organizations, including the American Academy of Pediatrics (healthychildren.org), the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), the World Health Organization (WHO), the American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists (ACOG), the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA), and Postpartum Support International.

What We Are Not

We are parents, not physicians. Nothing on this site is medical advice, and it should never replace a conversation with your pediatrician or healthcare provider. Read our full medical disclaimer.

Corrections

If you spot an error, please let us know via the contact form. We review reported issues and correct articles promptly.

Advertising & Affiliate Content

Advertising never influences our editorial recommendations. Affiliate links are disclosed in our Terms of Use, and we only link to products we would recommend regardless of commission.
